Same keyboard, two pipelines

Both at 351×114 mm, both in the scene's cabinet projection, both drawn with the identical line-weight hierarchy. The left is hand-authored SVG; the right is modelled in Blender and rendered through Freestyle with geometric hidden-line removal. The right one registers into the scene's own coordinate space via a single transform, because the model is sheared by the projection before an orthographic render — so a bake can sit in the real drawing unchanged.
