The Desk — block-out

Deliberately throwaway. Labelled boxes, correct positions and heights, camera path drawn over the top. The point is to settle where everything sits before any of it gets drawn properly — moving a box here costs a minute, moving a finished illustration costs a day.

◆ Rev 2 — seated chair, four objects added, colour rule revised

The colour rule

The desk stays a wireframe. No fills, no colour, just line. When a section comes into shot, the objects that section is about take their own real colours — paint in the pans, greens on the map, terracotta on the mug, blue ink on the whiteboard. Not a blanket yellow on everything.

Yellow goes back to being the brand colour in the interface — buttons, underlines, the progress bar — rather than being smeared across the illustration.

The world stays a sketch until you look closely. Then it becomes real.
