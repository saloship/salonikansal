No. 01July 2024

MacleodganjHimachal Pradesh

Camping in the mountains will always be my favourite kind of trip, and Triund is a fine place for it. An easy trail of grassland and rock, plenty of viewpoints, and majestic peaks sitting in the clouds in the distance. Dharamkot's market deserves a full day; it has a whole vibe of its own. We had less time for Dharamshala, but its market and monastery are a different cultural experience.

An easy trail where you find your own path.

Dharamkot Market

A market unlike any I'd seen.

03

A thirty-foot stream falling straight down.

04

Dharamshala

Monastery, stadium, and quiet tea gardens.

KasolParvati Valley

Parvati Valley is the kind of place that slows you right down — riverside cafés, pine air, and the hot springs at Manikaran. The Kheerganga trek rewards you with a hot spring at the very top; go slow, and let the valley set the pace.

No. 03March 2024

JibhiBanjar Valley

I went in late March, so it was cold — but only at night, perfect to explore by day and sit around a bonfire in the evening. Day two rained hard, so we mostly café-hopped, yet we still caught the feeling of being above the clouds; the Serolsar trek lay under fresh snow. Two nights covers the main spots — stretch to four to do every trek without rushing.

01

Choie Waterfall

A short trek, river and mountains alongside.

In the town, still wrapped in greenery.

04

Café hopping

Cafés made to work and linger in.

No. 04May 2024

ChoptaUttarakhand · Dev Bhumi

We stayed near the start of the Tungnath trek, so mornings were simple — just begin walking. The path is well paved, with straight shortcuts to jump. Chandrashila and back in a day is easy, though some stay at Tungnath for the sunrise. Deoriatal is gentle too — forest, cliff-edge viewpoints, and a lake of calm emerald water. Uttarakhand is called Dev Bhumi, and it truly felt like a divine memory.

01

Tungnath Trek 3,680 m

The world's highest Shiva temple, on to Chandrashila.

02

Deoriatal Lake 2,438 m

An emerald lake amid rhododendron forest.

03

Dhari Devi Temple

On the bank of the Alaknanda river.

No. 05January 2024

JaipurRajasthan

We had two and a half days — enough to see everything and still shop at the end. The markets are beautiful for clothes, bags, spices, all of it, and mostly within five to ten kilometres, so the day starts easily. Winter is the season; the city opens late and closes early. Timing is everything with the temples — they shut early, and the lines can be long.

01

The Palaces

City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Amber & Jal Mahal.

02

03

Museums

Albert Hall, the Wax Museum, gem & jewel.

04

Forts & Gates

Nahargarh, Jaigarh, Patrika Gate.

Munnar is all tea gardens and postcard sunsets, with Kochi's spice markets and backwaters to round it out. The boat ride at Mattupetty — hills mirrored in still water — is the calm I remember most.
